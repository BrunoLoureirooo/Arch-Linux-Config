Cyber Terminal Rice

A mock of the planned Quickshell desktop on your two screens: the BenQ on the left, the laptop panel on the right. Nothing is installed or changed. Everything shown is drawn by Quickshell or Hyprland with blur, shadows and easing still off. Wallpapers and values here are stand-ins.

Click a screen to focus it. 1–5 jump workspace, ←→ step (the mouse side buttons), Tab switch screen. Each screen keeps its own 1–5.

Pick a wallpaper and everything recolours: wallpaper, bar, rail, window borders and the page itself. On the real setup matugen writes the palette and Quickshell, kitty, btop and Hyprland reload from it. The cut corners are Quickshell surfaces (bar tile, rail, launcher, notifications, carousel). Hyprland cannot cut window corners, so windows keep its 1px gradient border, set by col.active_border.

Workspaces: one pool per screen

  • BenQ and laptop each get their own 1–5. Switching on one screen never touches the other, like Spaces on the Mac.
  • Mechanism: a short function in Hyprland's Lua config, no plugin. The BenQ owns workspaces 1–5 and the laptop panel owns 11–15; the keys always act on the screen you are on.
  • Your mouse is a Logitech G502 Hero. Its back/forward side buttons become previous/next workspace on the focused screen, as on the Mac. They stop doing back/forward in browsers.
  • Unverified: the exact button codes (expected mouse:275/276) and that a bind on a bare mouse button works as written. Both get checked first, before anything else is built on them.

  • The remap tool is xremap (AUR, Hyprland build). It matches on the focused app, so terminals can be treated differently. A global Super→Ctrl swap (keyd, or the XKB swap) would break Ctrl+C and Ctrl+W in kitty, so I am not using it.
  • A translated key press re-enters the compositor, so no workspace or window bind may equal a translated output. That is why stepping is Ctrl+Alt+←/→ and window focus is Super+Alt+arrows. A test checks the two files for overlap.
  • Your current app binds (Super+T/F/C/E/R/I) collide with these. They move to Ctrl+Alt+T/F/C/E/R/I, plus the launcher. This is a proposal; any row is easy to flip.
  • Unverified: xremap-hypr-bin builds and starts cleanly on this machine (one report of a slow start after login), and JetBrains and Electron apps pick up the remapped keys.

Stack and budget

  • Quickshell 0.3.1 (official repo) replaces waybar, wofi, mako and hyprpaper with one QML process: a bar on each screen, the right rail on the main screen, launcher, notification server, wallpaper layer and the carousel.
  • matugen 4.2.0 (official repo) turns the picked wallpaper into a palette file. No swww/awww is needed because Quickshell draws the wallpaper itself, which also fixes the BenQ having none today.
  • kitty gets a real config (it has none now), with the palette included from matugen's output. btop, nvim and Hyprland borders follow the same file.
  • Hyprland moves from the stub hyprland.conf to the Lua config that 0.56 ships, since that is the supported format on 0.56 and it lets the per-screen pools be a short function. Your lid, headless and monitor logic carry over unchanged.
